QuranOne Privacy Policy

Last updated: 10 October 2026

QuranOne is a free Quran reading and listening app developed by Abdiaziz Roble. It has no ads, no accounts and no analytics or tracking. This policy explains what happens to your information when you use it.

Information we collect

We do not collect, store or sell any personal information. The developer has no server that receives your data, and the app contains no analytics, advertising or tracking code.

Information kept on your device

The app keeps the following only on your phone, so it can work as you expect: your bookmarks, Hifz (memorisation) progress, last reading position, settings (such as text size, theme and reciter) and any recitations you choose to download. This information never leaves your device through the app. It is removed when you delete the app.

Recitation audio

When you play or download a recitation, the app requests the audio files from the servers that host them (EveryAyah.com and the app's own audio storage on Cloudflare). As with any web request, those servers receive your device's IP address and basic request information in order to deliver the file. The app sends no other information with these requests.

Lock screen and media controls

While a recitation plays, the app shows the surah, ayah and reciter on your lock screen and in the system media controls (Control Centre on iPhone, the media notification on Android), using the phone's standard features. This information stays on your device.

Children

The app is suitable for all ages and collects no information from anyone, including children.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
