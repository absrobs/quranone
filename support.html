QuranOne Support

QuranOne is a free Quran reading and listening app, with no ads and no tracking.

Contact

For questions, problems or suggestions, email Abdiaziz Roble at abdi.roble@outlook.com. If you have found a problem, please include your phone model and what you were doing when it happened.

Common questions

Recitation does not play. Recitations stream over the internet. Check your connection, or download the surah for offline listening (Library › Downloads).

How do I change the reciter? Tap the reciter's name at the bottom of the reader, or go to Settings › Reciter.

How do I scroll continuously instead of turning pages? Settings › Reading mode › Vertical.

The Hijri date is a day off. Settings › Hijri date adjustment lets you match your local moon sighting.
